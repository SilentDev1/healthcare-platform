# Pricing publication

Only deterministic, reviewed procedure mappings with acceptable source confidence and no unresolved blocking anomaly become publishable. Summaries combine only compatible facility, procedure, setting, component scope, payer, and plan observations. A range remains a range; no midpoint or missing value is invented.

Public displays must say that hospital-file prices may not equal a final bill, separate charges may apply, a negotiated rate does not establish network participation, and actual out-of-pocket cost depends on benefits and services received. No page offers patient-specific estimates or medical recommendations.
